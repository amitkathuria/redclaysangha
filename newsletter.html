Teacher's Note

Carrying the Practice Forward

Dear sangha,

Our 90-Day Ango draws to a close in early October, and the practices it asked of us—early rising, steady attention, showing up whether or not we felt like it—don't end with the practice period. They become the ground we stand on. This month we continue Working with Mind's exploration of Yogacara's fifty-one mental factors, now in its third session.

Midmonth brings a full day of practice at our One Day Retreat—a chance to stabilize and deepen without the commitment of an overnight stay. Our friends in the Sacred Friends interfaith series continue exploring Hinduism together, including two temple visits this month alongside the regular Zoom sessions.

And as the days shorten, we close out October with something lighter: a movie night built for connection rather than concentration. Whether you're sitting in silence or watching a film in community, it's the same practice—showing up fully for what's in front of us.

However you are arriving—rested or exhausted, joyful or struggling—you are welcome here.

With gratitude,
Gareth Young

Volunteer Opportunities – Community Service

Red Clay and Peach Blossom Sanghas collaborate on ongoing community service projects, bringing Buddhist values off the cushion and into the world through compassionate action.

Projects rotate between two partners: Nicholas House, which helps families experiencing homelessness achieve self-sufficiency, and, as its Nov 1–Mar 31 season begins, Bashor Homeless Men's Shelter, where volunteers prepare 30+ daytime lunch sacks ahead of time, then return to reheat, plate, and serve a meal on a Saturday evening.

A specific date for the next project had not yet been posted; please visit our Community Service page for the latest schedule. Friends, family, and newcomers are all welcome.
