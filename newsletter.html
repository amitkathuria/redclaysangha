Reflection for August
“Think not lightly of good, saying, ‘It will not come to me.’ Drop by drop is the water pot filled. Likewise, the wise man, gathering it little by little, fills himself with good.”
– Dhammapada, v. 122, trans. Acharya Buddharakkhita
Teacher's Note

Settling In: The Rains Retreat Season

Dear sangha,

We are now within Ango/Vassa, the traditional rains retreat season, when practitioners across the Buddhist world commit to settling in one place and practicing together with a little more steadiness than usual. Our own 90-Day Ango, led by Benjamin, gathers each Monday morning at 5:00 AM—an invitation to let one small, repeated act of dedication accumulate, drop by drop.

This August at Red Clay Sangha, we'll begin a new study of the fifty-one mental factors of Yogacara Buddhism, sit a full day of silence together, chant, share poetry with Clay Roper, and make room for laughter at a community game day.

I'm also glad to share that Tuesday mornings now have a new home: Katie Rhodes and Holly Theisen-Jones are co-facilitating our Tuesday Morning Meditation and Reflection, 6:15–6:55 AM on Zoom, a quiet forty minutes of sitting and reflection to open the day.

Our new WhatsApp community and website are both good places to keep up with what's coming, so please look there and join in.

However you are arriving—rested or exhausted, joyful or struggling—you are welcome here.

With gratitude,
Gareth Young

Working with Mind: The Fifty-One Mental Factors of Yogacara Buddhism

Saturday, August 1 • 9:30 AM – 12:00 PM • Red Clay Sangha & Zoom

Yogacara Buddhism mapped fifty-one mental factors that arise and combine to shape our moment-to-moment experience. This five-part series offers a hands-on approach to recognizing those factors as they arise—along with several contemporary ones—and to cultivating the wholesome while applying antidotes to the unwholesome. Talks are recorded, so you can catch up if you miss a session. Learn more and register here.
