Teacher's Note

Practicing Across Difference

Dear sangha,

Our 90-Day Ango continues, and with it the quiet discipline of showing up— Benjamin's Monday sits at 5:00 AM, the Working with Mind study of Yogacara's fifty-one mental factors, and in the middle of the month, a five-day silent retreat with me at YMCA Camp Ocoee, combining Chan practice and the Pali teachings.

September also turns us outward. We host a workshop on staying grounded and connected across political and social divides, and return to Nicholas House to cook and share a meal with families staying there.

Whether you're settling into stillness at a retreat or sitting across the table from someone who sees the world differently, it's the same practice: meeting what's in front of us with a little more openness than before.

However you are arriving—rested or exhausted, joyful or struggling—you are welcome here.

With gratitude,
Gareth Young

Five-Day Silent Retreat: Chan Practice and the Pali Teachings

Wednesday, September 16 – Sunday, September 20 • YMCA Camp Ocoee

A residential retreat with Gareth Young combining sitting and walking meditation, chanting, and silence, drawing on both the Pali suttas and Chan tradition. Open to experienced practitioners and those new to multi-day silent retreats alike—prior meditation experience is helpful but not required. Vegetarian cuisine is served, with vegan, dairy-free, and gluten-free options at every meal, and scholarships are available for those needing financial assistance. There are still spaces available. Learn more and register here.

Working with Mind: The Fifty-One Mental Factors of Yogacara Buddhism

Saturday, September 5 • 9:30 AM – 12:00 PM • Red Clay Sangha & Zoom

Yogacara Buddhism mapped fifty-one mental factors that arise and combine to shape our moment-to-moment experience. This five-part series, now in its second session, offers a hands-on approach to recognizing those factors as they arise and to cultivating the wholesome while applying antidotes to the unwholesome. Talks are recorded, so you can catch up if you miss a session. Learn more and register here.

Resilience: Navigating a Troubled World

Saturday, September 26 • 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM • In person and Zoom

Many of us are concerned over the increasing discord and polarization within our communities and even families, especially around the upcoming election. It's tempting to retreat into a closed circle of those who share our views, but distancing ourselves only deepens the divisions. This session offers guidelines and a best-practices approach to bridging divisions between ourselves and others—a non-violent, non-partisan approach based on initiatives sponsored by the Carter Center. Learn more and register here.
