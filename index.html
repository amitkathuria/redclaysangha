Reflection for August
“Think not lightly of good, saying, ‘It will not come to me.’ Drop by drop is the water pot filled. Likewise, the wise man, gathering it little by little, fills himself with good.”
– Dhammapada, v. 122, trans. Acharya Buddharakkhita
Teacher's Note

Settling In: The Rains Retreat Season

Dear sangha,

We are now within Ango/Vassa, the traditional rains retreat season, when practitioners across the Buddhist world commit to settling in one place and practicing together with a little more steadiness than usual. Our own 90-Day Ango, led by Benjamin, gathers each Monday morning at 5:00 AM—an invitation to let one small, repeated act of dedication accumulate, drop by drop.

This August at Red Clay Sangha, we'll begin a new study of the fifty-one mental factors of Yogacara Buddhism, sit a full day of silence together, chant, share poetry with Clay Roper, and make room for laughter at a community game day.

However you are arriving—rested or exhausted, joyful or struggling—you are welcome here.

With gratitude,
Gareth Young
