WELCOME TO RED CLAY SANGHA

3315 Chamblee Dunwoody Road, Chamblee GA 30341

We're a pluralist, peer-led Buddhist community offering meditation, mindfulness, and dharma practice for over 15 years.

Our primary weekly gathering is the Sunday morning service from 9:00 to 11:30. We also offer retreats, classes, and other recurring weekly programs — see our Calendar for the full schedule, or Programs to learn more. No experience is necessary to attend any of our events.

You can attend most of our programs either in person or online on Zoom.
