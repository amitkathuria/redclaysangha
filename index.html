Teacher's Note

Summer as a Field of Practice

Dear sangha,

In these bright, long days, our lives may feel especially full—of family, travel, work, news, and the many joys and challenges of being in community. Rather than waiting for a “perfect” quiet moment to practice, we are invited to notice that our whole life is the field of practice.

This July at Red Clay Sangha, we'll continue to explore the foundations of the path through Sunday talks, weekly sits, and several special offerings. We'll also deepen our relationships through study, chanting, service, and simple time spent together.

However you are arriving—rested or exhausted, joyful or struggling—you are welcome here.

With gratitude,
Red Clay Sangha Teachers and Leaders
